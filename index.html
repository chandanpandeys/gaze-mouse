Low-stress calibration
The system uses the whole face, both eye shapes, eyelids, iris position, blink/look blendshapes and head pose. Calibration uses up to 54 points, but it gives you short blink/rest breaks and automatically repeats only bad points. You do not need to force your eyes open.

Fix the phone in portrait, roughly 35–45 cm away. Keep your face centered and use front lighting. Look naturally at the dot; small head movement is allowed because head pose is modeled. Blink during the short rest between points, not while a point is sampling.
