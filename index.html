How to get the best result
1. Fix the phone in one position, portrait, 35–45 cm from your face.
2. Put light in front of you, not behind you. Keep both eyes fully visible.
3. During calibration move only your eyes. Do not move the phone or your head.
4. When a green dot appears, first move your eyes to it, then hold your gaze. Avoid blinking while it samples. If you blink or move, that point repeats automatically.
5. Use Dwell first. Only test wink/double-blink after the cursor is accurate.
