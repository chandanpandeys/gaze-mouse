Looking is not clicking.
Gaze only moves the pointer. Selection requires your trained fast double blink, and actions only work while Eye control is ON. Eye control starts OFF. Hold a left wink for about half a second to toggle it. Hold a right wink while control is on to switch between Pointer and Scroll mode.

Gaze calibration uses five comfortable horizontal paths and three deliberately slower vertical paths. No diagonals. Each path holds still first and ignores its first 500 ms of motion so your eyes can catch up before training samples are recorded.
