Calibration is deliberately slow now.
After you tap calibration, the target does not move immediately. You get a prepare screen, then a practice sweep, then the real calibration. Every real sweep first holds still at its starting point before it moves. The moving target is much slower, and calibration compensates for the natural delay between the target moving and your eyes following it.

Keep the phone fixed in portrait, roughly 35–45 cm away. Follow the green dot naturally; do not chase it. Blink whenever needed — blink frames are ignored. If your eyes lose it, simply find the dot again; the calibration never waits for a perfect frame.
